What are the Echoes of the Nile?

An echo of the Nile is a story that keeps travelling: a myth or a memory from Egypt and the Mediterranean, told again and again until it reaches you.

Each tale on this site has a short summary and a longer retelling, so you can read it in one minute or take your time with it.
